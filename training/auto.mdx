`/v1/training/auto` is the private-access autonomous path for the Training API.
You provide the training objective and success criteria; Fastino's agent plans
and runs the training lifecycle.

The agent can coordinate:

- Dataset preparation and validation
- Candidate training and evaluation
- Questions and approval checkpoints
- Progress against a measurable success criterion
- Delivery of models that meet the approved bar

<Note>
  Training Auto is in private access and is not included in the public OpenAPI
  or API reference.
</Note>

This page intentionally does not publish endpoints or request schemas while the
private contract is evolving.
