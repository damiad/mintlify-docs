Fastino provides APIs for specialized inference and model training. Use
**GLiDE** for classification, routing, and scoring with calibrated
probabilities, or **GLiNER** for span-level extraction. With the
[Training API](/training), you can run managed fine-tuning, control a custom
training loop, or delegate the training lifecycle to private-access Auto Train.

## Supported models

### Inference

- **GLiDE** for decisions: classification, routing, and scoring with calibrated probabilities instead of generated text.
- **GLiNER-2.5-Decide** for English classification: intent, routing, sentiment, priority, moderation, and ordinal scoring.
- **GLiNER 2.5 Multilingual** for multilingual structured extraction.
- **GLiNER2 and GLiGuard** for entity extraction, classification, structured data, safety, and PII workflows.

- **GLiNER 2.5 Multilingual** for multilingual structured extraction.
- **GLiNER2 and GLiGuard** for entity extraction, classification, structured data, safety, and PII workflows.
- **Nemotron 3.5 Lightning** in general-purpose, Healthcare, and Finance variants.

### Training

| Workflow | Models |
| --- | --- |
| Managed encoder training | Trainable GLiNER models returned by the live catalog |
| Interactive decoder training | Trainable decoder models returned by the live catalog |

Call `GET /v1/base-models?supports_training=true` for current training
availability and capabilities; availability can vary by team. See
[Models](/concepts/models) for the model catalog.
